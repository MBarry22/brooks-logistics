Services

Courier and expedited freight in one cargo van, driven direct from pickup to delivery. No terminals and no transfers.

Same-day local delivery

Pickup and delivery on the same business day anywhere in our Middle Georgia service area.

Your shipment goes straight from pickup to drop-off without stopping at a terminal or sharing a route with other freight.

Good for: replacement parts, boxed and palletized freight, contracts and documents.

Expedited regional runs

Direct runs to anywhere in the US, mostly within 300 to 400 miles of Macon. We take longer trips for the right load.

A dedicated van means your freight is the only stop on the trip.

Good for: time-sensitive loads that can’t wait for LTL or a parcel carrier.

Medical and pharmacy deliveries

Prescriptions, clinic supplies and other medical deliveries between pharmacies, clinics and offices.

If your delivery has special handling requirements, include them in your quote request so we can confirm before booking.

Good for: pharmacies, clinics, labs and medical offices.

On-call and recurring routes

Call us when something needs to move, or set up a regular route we run on the same days each week.

Recurring routes are billed per run, so there’s no driver on payroll.

Good for: businesses with regular deliveries between locations, suppliers or customers.
