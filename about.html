About Brooks Logistics & Transport

A Georgia courier and expedited freight company built on careful handling and on-time delivery.

Our background

Brooks Logistics & Transport is owned and operated by Tate Brooks. Tate spent nine years at UPS, five of them as a driver, before starting the company in Juliette, Georgia.

That experience shapes how we work: freight is handled carefully, pickups happen when we say they will, and deliveries are confirmed.

We bill per run as a 1099 contractor, so businesses get a dependable carrier without adding a driver to payroll or paying benefits.

Authority and insurance

Brooks Logistics & Transport is fully insured and operates under its own authority. You can verify our USDOT and MC numbers on the FMCSA website.
